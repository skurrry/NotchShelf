# Roadmap

The roadmap is not binding and may change during alpha development.

## Alpha stabilization

- Harden shelf persistence and drag-and-drop workflows
- Test Quick Look thumbnails and cache behavior
- Stabilize timer, alarm, snooze, and compact timer states
- Refine top-down notch animations without visual gaps
- Improve accessibility, keyboard navigation, and Reduce Motion support
- Test clean builds from a fresh clone

## First public alpha

- Add neutral screenshots and a short demo
- Document known limitations
- Publish source tag `v0.1.0-alpha.1`
- Prepare an optional signed and notarized DMG

## Future possibilities

- Calendar integration
- More customizable compact information pages
- Automated tests and CI
- Additional localizations
- Update mechanism
- Performance and energy optimization
